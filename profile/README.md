## We build systems that must tell the truth

KeTeKe Labs is a product and engineering company creating dependable digital platforms for organisations operating in complex environments. We work where identity, commercial rules, operational events and accountability must remain understandable from the first decision to the final outcome.

Our approach is grounded in a simple conviction:

> A system should never claim more than it can prove.

That conviction shapes how we design products, define boundaries, record evidence and operate software. We favour explicit rules, deterministic behaviour and architectures that can survive implementation, failure and change.

## How we engineer

### Zero Assumptions

We make important system facts explicit. Identity, authority, tenancy, ownership, state and commercial meaning should not depend on silent conventions.

### Deterministic Agentic Design

We use automation and AI within governed processes. Agents may accelerate the work, but the system must preserve clear inputs, constrained actions, observable outcomes and verifiable evidence.

### Evidence before confidence

A successful deployment is not automatically a successful product journey. We test the behaviour that customers and operators depend on, and preserve evidence of what actually happened.

### Products reveal architecture

Architecture becomes credible when it survives contact with real journeys, real failure and real change. We allow product experience to challenge assumptions and sharpen our platform boundaries.

## What we work with

Our engineering work spans Java, TypeScript, Python and Dart, with cloud infrastructure, APIs, event-driven systems, automated testing, delivery pipelines and production operations forming part of the same product discipline.

We are particularly interested in systems that require:

- dependable commercial and operational rules;
- clear identity, tenancy and authorisation boundaries;
- auditable workflows and evidence;
- reusable capabilities without loss of product meaning;
- resilient deployment and release practices;
- responsible use of AI in software delivery and operations.
